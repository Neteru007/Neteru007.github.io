# Content provenance

## Demons

- Appearance, offices, ranks and legions: S. L. MacGregor Mathers / Aleister Crowley, *The Goetia* (1904), records 1–72; public-domain text at https://sacred-texts.com/book/the-lesser-key-of-solomon/shemhamphorash . Ukrainian descriptions are editorial paraphrases. Variant counts such as Bifrons 6 and Volac 38 follow this edition.
- Glyphs: kvgrimorium/ars-goetia-svg-sigils, commit 1edfedbbb014ec03dc6760cce80d9205c08adcc4, CC0. See https://grimorium.lat/en/seal-license/ .
- 35 historical illustrations: public-domain scans with individual Wikimedia Commons source pages in each record. Most are Louis Le Breton / M. Jarrault, *Dictionnaire infernal* (1863), a demonological dictionary rather than the Goetia manuscript. Belial is a woodcut from *Buch Belial* (1473), a theological-literary work. Cerbère/Naberius, Zaebos/Sallos and other differing iconographies are explicitly annotated. Unverified Sabnock artwork and a misidentified Ronwe file were excluded. Missing portraits are explicitly labeled.
- Zodiac decans: Crowley, *Liber 777*, columns CLV–CLXVI, https://thelema.tools/bot-books/aleister_crowley/libers/Liber.777.vel.QBL.of.AC.pdf . Numbers 1–36 are day spirits, 37–72 night spirits; each group has three spirits per sign. Element is derived from the sign, not asserted as a universal property of the spirit.
- Planetary rank mapping is stated separately. Earls use the hermetic Mars/iron attribution; the alternative copper/silver and editorial iron proposal in the 1904 *Observations* are noted. Furcas uses Saturn/lead from *Observations*. Belial's Earth correspondence in modern Demonolatry is separately noted, not conflated with Aquarius/air in 777.
- Enns: *Goetic Hierarchy Enns*, courtesy of Valerie Corban, https://demonolatry.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/goetic.pdf . The archive at https://demonolatry.org/library-archives/ explicitly allows its reference materials to be downloaded, shared and distributed freely. Original spelling is retained, including Glasya-Lobolas and Haaventi in that list. These are modern Demonolatry invocations, not text from the 1904 Goetia and not universally official across traditions.

## Angels

- Names, attributes, quoted English translations and Latin verse references: Lazare Lenain, *La Science Cabalistique* (1823), via https://magick.ly/kabbalah/yhvh/72angels and https://magick.ly/books/la-science-cabalistique . Lenain's historical text is public domain. Magick.ly's transcription, translation and arrangement are CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The site attributes Magick.ly and identifies Ukrainian editorial translations / abridgments.
- Zodiac scheme is Lenain's Aries-first sequence of 72 five-degree sectors. Decan planets follow the Chaldean order from Mars. Choirs are nine groups of eight. Psalms retain the source's Latin numbering, with Genesis 1:1 for Jabamiah; no demonolatry enns are invented for angels.
- All 72 seal images: Piers Vaughan, *Rosicrucian Sigils of the 72 Geniuses* (2020), https://rosecirclebooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Roscrucian-Sigils-of-the-72-Geniuses.pdf . The author explicitly permits unrestricted use and amendment at https://rosecirclebooks.com/the-science-of-the-kabbalah-lenain/ (paragraph beginning “So for those who wish to avail themselves…”). These are modern Rosicrucian reconstructions, not portraits and not facsimiles of an ancient grimoire. Each SVG contains a cropped PNG render of the matching numbered PDF page. The original name and seal have been preserved. Author and permission source are linked in every angel record.
- Ukrainian descriptions now include a summary of the historical offices, not just a translation of the divine attribute. The full quoted English translation remains available with Magick.ly attribution.
- Existing magic_gallery Hebrew-name medallions remain labeled as original modern design and are shown separately from the sourced seal.

## Completion of image coverage

All 144 records now have a source-backed visual. The 35 demon illustrations are retained. For 37 demons without a verified portrait, the image is the beginning of the numbered record in an electronic reprint of the public-domain 1904 text: https://biglevi.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/The-Book-Of-The-Goetia-Of-Solomon-The-King-A-Crowley-1904.pdf . These images are explicitly labeled **document excerpts**, not portraits and not scans of original 1904 printed pages. PDF page numbers and direct source links are preserved. They are also visually and semantically separated by the catalogue's image-type filter.

No missing historical portraits or universally official enns have been invented. The invocation traditions remain identified as above.


## Enochian section

`/#/enochian` uses the user-supplied scan of Gerald & Betty Schueler, *The Enochian Workbook: A Complete Guide to Angelic Magic*. The 19 English calls are from lesson 17, printed pp. 115–120; usage associations follow pp. 120–125. Ukrainian annotations are editorial summaries, not translations of the Enochian language. Five PNGs in `public/artwork/enochian/` reproduce figures 1–5, printed pp. 9–13 (PDF pp. 27–31), directly from the supplied scan. The Golden Dawn interpretation is identified separately from the original Dee/Kelley material.


## Ars Notoria section

## Rune guide

`/#/divination/runes` combines the existing one-rune draw with a 24-rune guide, search, three positional aett groups, historical background and original reflective readings. The same page is linked from Theurgy. No claim is made that the modern interpretive prompts reproduce a documented Viking divination method.

- National Museum of Denmark, writing-system background: https://natmus.dk/historisk-viden/temaer/runer/hvad-er-runer/
- Elder/Younger rows: https://natmus.dk/historisk-viden/temaer/runer/runer-i-jernalderen/
- Reconstructed names and literal meanings: https://natmus.dk/historisk-viden/temaer/runer/runernes-navne/ . Common Latin forms in the UI have spelling variants; they are not all verbatim museum spellings. Perthro, Kenaz/Kaunan, Algiz and Eihwaz receive uncertainty/variant notes.
- Literary vs inscriptional magic: https://en.natmus.dk/historical-knowledge/denmark/prehistoric-period-until-1050-ad/the-viking-age/religion-magic-death-and-rituals/runic-magic/
- B. Dickins (1915), Old English Rune Poem, primary text and edition: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Runic_and_Heroic_Poems_of_the_Old_Teutonic_Peoples/The_Runic_Poems/The_Anglo-Saxon_Runic_Poem . Referenced for name-image differences, not conflated with an Elder Futhark poem.
- Noto Sans Runic, unmodified font, SIL OFL 1.1: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/tree/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSansRunic . License distributed in `public/fonts/NotoSansRunic-OFL.txt`.

Rune guide symbols join the existing deck by symbol, keeping draw names and reflection questions consistent with the catalogue.


## Seven Arts detail pages (uploaded book)

`/#/ars-notoria/{grammar,logic,rhetoric,arithmetic,geometry,music,astronomy}` use the user-supplied *Ars Notoria: The Grimoire of Rapid Learning by Magic with the Golden Flowers of Apollonius of Tyana*, Vol. I — Version A, Robert Turner translation, edited and introduced by Stephen Skinner & Daniel Clark, Golden Hoard Press, 2019. The two supplied files, `ArsNotoria.pdf` and `ArsNotoria 2.pdf`, are byte-identical (SHA-256 2bfad979962f0429156f0c5d1c4aa6d912ae2359b4b4a9e3c8b983903b029c1a), so both identify one edition, not independent sources.

Ukrainian subject descriptions, context, prayers summaries and notae explanations are original paraphrases. Modern learning exercises are explicitly editorial additions. Each detail page records printed and PDF references. General curriculum: printed pp. 13–14 (PDF 19–20); notae discussion: 87–92 (PDF 93–98); practical context/gaps: 117–126 (PDF 123–132); subject text: 194–208 (PDF 200–214). Music's incomplete procedural coverage and astronomy's attribution conflicts are preserved, rather than filled with invented rituals.
